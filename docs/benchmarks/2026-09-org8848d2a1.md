# Request-engine benchmark: org8848d2a1

Date: 2026-09-10  
Environment: `org8848d2a1.crm4.dynamics.com`  
Scenario: 50 read-only `WhoAmI` requests after one warm-up request

| Requested MaxDop | Elapsed | Requests/second | Successful |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2.477 s | 20.18 | 50/50 |
| 4 | 0.623 s | 80.31 | 50/50 |
| 8 | 0.612 s | 81.66 | 50/50 |
| 16 | 0.504 s | 99.25 | 50/50 |
| 32 | 0.337 s | 148.21 | 50/50 |

Repeated runs were reported to be consistent within milliseconds. This workload benefits substantially from parallel individual requests and produced no errors through a requested DOP of 32. The current default ceiling of 20 remains conservative while still capturing most of the observed gain; Dataverse's `x-ms-dop-hint` can lower the effective concurrency.

This result does not establish defaults for mutating `$batch` requests or Dataverse bulk APIs. Those require a controlled disposable dataset, equivalent payloads, and cleanup verification before comparison.
